Privacy Policy — Islamic Trivia

Effective date: 8 November 2026
Publisher: Roomi Nizar
Privacy contact: roominizar@gmail.com

1. Introduction

Islamic Trivia — Journey of the Prophets is an educational mobile application published by Roomi Nizar. It helps users learn about the Prophets through educational stories, interactive quizzes, achievement badges, and learning progress tracking. We aim to minimize the information needed to use the application.

2. Accounts and Personal Information

Islamic Trivia does not require users to create an account, register, or log in. The app does not ask users to submit their name, email address, telephone number, or other contact details to use its educational features.

3. Learning Progress and Local Storage

To support its features, Islamic Trivia stores the following information locally on the device:

  • Experience points (XP) and learning levels
  • Completed stories and quizzes
  • Quiz scores and saved quiz progress
  • Achievement badges
  • Learning progress and application preferences

The current app is not designed to synchronize this information with an online account or upload learning progress to a developer-operated server.

4. Advertising and Analytics

Islamic Trivia does not display third-party advertisements and does not intentionally integrate dedicated third-party advertising trackers, analytics, or crash-reporting services. Underlying operating-system and application technologies may process technical information independently of these features. We will update this policy if the application's data practices change or further disclosures are required.

5. Data Sharing

Islamic Trivia is not designed to sell or share locally stored learning progress with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Retention and Deletion

Learning progress remains stored on the device until the app's local data is cleared or the app is uninstalled, subject to applicable device backup and restoration settings. Users can generally clear local app data through Android's application storage settings.

7. Intended Audience

Islamic Trivia is intended for teenagers aged 13 and above and adults. Users do not need to provide personal information to access its learning activities.

8. Security

We seek to minimize personal information required by the application. Locally stored application data is subject to the security protections of the device and operating system. No electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the application, its data practices, or applicable requirements. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact Roomi Nizar at roominizar@gmail.com.
